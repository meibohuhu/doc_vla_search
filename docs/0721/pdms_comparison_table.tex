\section*{GRPO Reward with CoT Length Penalty}

The reward is the PDM score of the rolled-out trajectory, reduced by a
chain-of-thought length penalty that only applies when the model invokes
slow, deliberate reasoning (i.e.\ when the completion text contains the
\texttt{"complex scenario"} trigger phrase):
\begin{align*}
R = R_{\text{pdm}}(\tau, y) \;-\; w_{\text{cot}} \cdot
    \sigma\big(k \, (\ell - c)\big) \cdot \mathbb{1}[\text{"complex scenario"} \in y]
\end{align*}
where $R_{\text{pdm}}$ is the PDM score of the predicted trajectory $\tau$ given
completion $y$, $\ell = |y|$ is the character length of the generated
completion, $c$ is the penalty center, $k$ is the sigmoid steepness
coefficient, and $w_{\text{cot}}$ is the penalty weight
($c = 400$, $k = 0.002$, $w_{\text{cot}} = 0.3$ by default). The sigmoid keeps
the penalty near $0$ for short completions and saturates toward
$w_{\text{cot}}$ as $\ell \gg c$, giving a smooth rather than a hard cutoff.

The group-relative advantage is then computed over $G$ rollouts sampled per
prompt:
\begin{align*}
A_i = \frac{R_i - \mathrm{mean}(\{R_j\}_{j=1}^{G})}{\mathrm{std}(\{R_j\}_{j=1}^{G}) + \epsilon}
\end{align*}
and the per-token GRPO loss combines the clipped/importance-weighted policy
term with a $k_3$ KL penalty against the reference model:
\begin{align*}
\mathcal{L}
  = -\, \mathbb{E}_t \Big[
      \tfrac{\pi_\theta(y_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(y_t)} \, A_i
      \;-\; \beta \Big( e^{\Delta_t} - \Delta_t - 1 \Big)
    \Big], \qquad
\Delta_t = \log \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_t) - \log \pi_\theta(y_t)
\end{align*}
